2. Orders & acceptance

When you place an order, you're making an offer to buy the items in your bag. We accept that offer when we send an order confirmation email. Before then, no contract exists and we may decline an order — for example if an item is mispriced, out of stock, or we suspect fraud.

You're responsible for making sure the delivery address and email address you give us are correct. If you spot a mistake, contact us within one hour of ordering and we'll do our best to correct it before the parcel leaves the workshop.

Age and capacity

You must be at least 18 years old, or have permission from a parent or guardian, to place an order. Some tools have sharp edges and are not toys; adult supervision is required for use by younger gardeners.

3. Prices & payment

Prices are shown in US dollars and include all applicable sales tax where required. Shipping is calculated at checkout; standard carbon-neutral shipping is free on orders over $75. We may change prices at any time, but the price you see at checkout is the price that applies to your order.

We accept major credit and debit cards, PayPal and Apple Pay. Payments are processed by our payment providers, not stored on our servers. If a payment is declined, we'll let you know and hold your order for 48 hours.

4. Shipping & delivery

We ship from Portland, Oregon, on business days. Typical delivery times are:

  • United States: 3–6 business days after dispatch.
  • Canada: 6–10 business days after dispatch.
  • International: 10–18 business days after dispatch, depending on customs.

Orders over $75 ship free within the United States. Delivery estimates aren't guarantees. If your parcel is late, lost or damaged in transit, tell us within 30 days of the estimated delivery date and we'll replace it or refund you. Any import duties or taxes charged by your country are your responsibility.

We pack plastic-free, in recycled board and paper tape, and offset the emissions of every shipment.

5. Returns & the 30-day in-the-dirt trial

You have 30 days from delivery to try your tools in the garden. If they're not right for you, send them back for a full refund — even if they've been used, as long as they're complete and not damaged beyond normal garden wear.

  • Email hello@jamesgarden.com with your order number to start a return.
  • We'll send a prepaid return label for US orders. International return postage is your responsibility unless the item is faulty.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of the tools arriving back with us.
  • Washed, altered, or heavily damaged items may be refused or partially refunded.
  • Personalised or made-to-order items can only be returned if faulty.

This trial is in addition to your statutory rights. If an item is faulty on arrival, we'll always cover return postage and offer a repair, replacement or refund.

6. Lifetime repair promise

Every jamesgarden tool carries a lifetime repair promise. If a tool fails in normal garden use because of a defect in materials or workmanship, we'll repair it free of charge for as long as the tool is in use — and we stock spare parts for every model we've ever made.

This promise covers manufacturing faults, including handles that split, springs that fail, and joints that work loose. It doesn't cover:

  • Rust or wear caused by leaving tools out in the rain or soil.
  • Damage from misuse, modification, or use as a pry bar, hammer or chisel.
  • Cosmetic marks that don't affect how the tool works.
  • Sharpening, which is normal maintenance (though our guides will show you how).

To claim, email a photo of the tool and your order number, or bring it to the workshop. We'll repair it and cover return shipping within the US. If we can't repair it, we'll replace it with the nearest equivalent.

We make tools in small runs, so colours, wood grain and finishes vary slightly from the photographs. Those variations are part of buying something made by hand. Measurements and weights are approximate.

We try to keep the site accurate, but stock changes quickly. If an item you ordered is unavailable, we'll contact you and offer a substitute, a backorder, or a full refund. We never substitute an item without asking.
