1. Overview

This policy explains what personal information jamesgarden collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies whenever you visit jamesgarden.com, buy something from us, join our mailing list or contact us.

The short version: we use your details to fulfil orders and answer emails, use a few cookies to keep the site working, and send marketing only if you ask us to. You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time.

2. Who we are

jamesgarden Tools Co. ("jamesgarden", "we", "us") is the data controller for the information described in this policy. We're based at 412 SE Division Street, Portland, Oregon 97202, USA. For privacy matters, contact privacy@jamesgarden.com.

3. What we collect

We collect information you give us directly, including:

  • Order details: your name, billing and delivery address, email address, phone number, and the items you buy.
  • Account details: your email address, password (stored securely by our platform), and order history if you create an account.
  • Payment details: handled by our payment providers. We see the last four digits of your card and the payment result, but never your full card number.
  • Correspondence: emails, repair requests, reviews and messages you send us, including any photos.
  • Marketing preferences: whether you've opted in to our newsletter and which emails you've opened.
  • Plan downloads: the email address you use to receive a free plan, and which plan you requested.

You can choose not to provide some information, but we may not be able to process an order or repair request without it.

4. Information collected automatically

When you visit the site, our servers and analytics tools record limited technical information, including your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, the pages you view and how long you stay. We use this in aggregate to understand what's working and to keep the site secure.

We don't use this information to identify you personally unless we need to investigate fraud or a security incident.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use them to:

  • Keep the shop working: remember your bag, checkout session and login. These are essential and can't be switched off.
  • Remember preferences: such as your region or dismissed notices.
  • Measure traffic: understand which pages and guides are useful, using aggregated analytics.
  • Measure marketing: if you arrive from a newsletter link, understand whether the campaign was useful. We don't use advertising cookies to follow you around other sites.

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may stop checkout from working. Where required by law, we'll ask for your consent before setting non-essential cookies.

6. How we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • Process orders, take payment, pack and deliver your tools.
  • Handle returns, repairs, warranty claims and spare-parts requests.
  • Respond to questions and provide customer support.
  • Send order confirmations, dispatch notices and service messages.
  • Send our newsletter and free plans, where you've asked for them.
  • Improve the site, our products and our guides.
  • Prevent fraud, enforce our terms and meet legal obligations.

We don't use your personal information for automated decision-making that has a legal or similarly significant effect on you.

7. Legal bases for processing

If you're in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Contract: to fulfil an order or repair request you've made.
  • Legitimate interests: to run and secure the site, prevent fraud, and improve our products, balanced against your rights.
  • Consent: for newsletter marketing and non-essential cookies. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation: to keep tax and transaction records, or respond to lawful requests.

8. How we share your information

We share only what's necessary with service providers who help us run the shop, including:

  • Shop platform and hosting: to run the store and keep it online.
  • Payment processors: to take and refund payments securely.
  • Delivery carriers: to deliver your order and provide tracking.
  • Email and analytics providers: to send service messages, newsletters and aggregated traffic reports.
  • Professional advisers and authorities: where required by law, to collect a debt, or to protect our rights.

These providers may only use your information to provide services to us. We don't sell or rent your personal information, and we don't share it with advertisers for their own marketing.

9. Marketing choices

We send marketing only when you've opted in. Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link, and you can also email privacy@jamesgarden.com to be removed. We'll action your request promptly and no later than 30 days.

Unsubscribing from marketing doesn't stop essential messages about an order, repair or legal matter.

10. International transfers

We're based in the United States, and some of our service providers process data there. If you're in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, we use appropriate safeguards for transfers, such as standard contractual clauses, and require our providers to protect your information to a comparable standard.

11. How long we keep your information

We keep order and payment records for at least seven years where tax and accounting law requires it. Repair records are kept for as long as we honour the lifetime repair promise. Marketing preferences are kept until you unsubscribe. Analytics data is retained in aggregate for up to 26 months.

When information is no longer needed, we delete it or anonymise it securely.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Delete information we no longer need, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Portability: receive a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Object or restrict processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent to marketing or non-essential cookies at any time.
  • Complain to your local data protection authority.

To exercise a right, email privacy@jamesgarden.com. We'll verify your identity and respond within 30 days (or 45 days for California requests, where permitted). We won't charge you or treat you differently for making a privacy request.

13. California privacy rights

If you're a California resident, the CCPA/CPRA gives you the right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose; to request deletion or correction; to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information; and to limit the use of sensitive personal information. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by California law, and we do not use sensitive information for purposes beyond providing our services.

To make a request, email privacy@jamesgarden.com with "California request" in the subject line. You may use an authorised agent; we may ask for proof of authorisation.

14. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls, and vetted service providers to protect your information. No online service is completely secure, but we work to keep our systems current and to limit the data we hold. If a breach affects your rights, we'll notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

15. Children

Our shop is intended for adults. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in some jurisdictions). If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our shop or the law changes. We'll post the new version here and update the date at the top. If the change is significant, we'll let newsletter subscribers know by email.

17. Contact us

For any privacy question or request, email privacy@jamesgarden.com, or write to jamesgarden Tools Co., 412 SE Division Street, Portland, OR 97202, USA. You can also contact our EU representative at eu@jamesgarden.com.
